Oktay Kartal

DESIGNER · RESEARCHER

Oktay Kartal is a designer currently pursuing a Master of Architecture at North Carolina State University. He received his Bachelor of Architecture from Middle East Technical University.

His current collaborative research examines how everyday practices, institutional histories, and social life shape space. Alongside this work, he is developing interests in computation, simulation, artificial intelligence, and immersive environments, particularly through interdisciplinary and collaborative approaches to design.
